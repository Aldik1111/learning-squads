# First French conversation — 15 minutes

Agree to use polite `vous` for this exercise unless both partners prefer otherwise. Fictional first names are welcome. Ask before giving corrections.

## Minutes 0–3: greet and listen
Take turns saying `Bonjour. Je m’appelle... Et vous ?`. Listen to the answer and repeat the name you heard, using a fictional one if preferred.

## Minutes 3–7: ask for repetition
Practice `Pouvez-vous répéter, s’il vous plaît ?`. The partner repeats more slowly. Switch roles. Say `Je parle un peu français` to explain your current level without apologizing repeatedly.

## Minutes 7–12: build a complete exchange
Start again with no script for the first two lines. Include a greeting, both introductions, a request for repetition, a thank-you, and a goodbye. Repeat with roles reversed and a different fictional name.

## Minutes 12–15: feedback and reflection
Ask which phrase was easiest to understand and choose one pronunciation point to improve. Repeat that phrase in the full exchange. Write your own six-line version afterward; do not record or identify your partner.

Aim for at least 15 minutes of practice for this module, which can accumulate across sessions.
