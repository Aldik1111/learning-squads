# French daily-plans conversation — 15 minutes

Use fictional activities and times. Keep polite `vous` consistent unless both partners agree to another form.

## Minutes 0–3: two plans
Each speaker says one plan for today and one for tomorrow using `Je vais...`. The listener identifies which plan belongs to tomorrow.

## Minutes 3–7: ask for details
Take turns with `Qu’est-ce que vous allez faire demain ?` and `À quelle heure ?`. Include morning or evening. Ask for repetition if the time is unclear.

## Minutes 7–12: revise an arrangement
Propose a study time at ten. The partner asks to change it to eleven. Confirm the new time and activity. Swap roles, change the activity, and repeat. Clarify real-world time-zone concepts in a shared language if needed.

## Minutes 12–15: recall and correction
Say the final plan without reading. Ask your partner to help with one verb or time phrase. Write a fictional exchange and note the correction in your own task result.
