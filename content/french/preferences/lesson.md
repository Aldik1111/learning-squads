# Choose an activity in French

A conversation about preferences works best when both people get a turn. Learn to state what you like, ask about the other person's choice, and find an activity together. The goal is communication, not matching opinions.

## Useful phrases

- `J’aime lire.` — I like reading.
- `J’aime écouter de la musique.` — I like listening to music.
- `Je préfère marcher.` — I prefer walking.
- `Je n’aime pas courir.` — I do not like running.
- `Vous préférez lire ou marcher ?` — Do you prefer reading or walking?
- `D’accord.` — Agreed / okay.

For these activity sentences, keep the activity verb in its infinitive form after `aime` or `préfère`. The apostrophe in `j’aime` replaces the vowel of `je` before a vowel sound. In the written negative pattern here, `ne` becomes `n’` before `aime`, and `pas` follows the conjugated verb.

## Try an exchange

A: `J’aime lire. Vous préférez lire ou marcher ?`

B: `Je préfère marcher aujourd’hui.`

A: `D’accord. J’aime marcher aussi.`

B: `Très bien.`

The preference can be specific to today. Do not interpret a different choice as a criticism. If you dislike the suggestion, say so respectfully and offer another familiar activity. You may practice with invented preferences rather than disclose personal details.

## Practice

Write two likes and one dislike, then build an either-or question. With a partner, try an exchange where your first choices differ and negotiate a shared alternative. In your task result, show a fictional dialogue and explain one phrase you improved. Reading the words silently is preparation; use the conversation guide to practice listening and responding aloud when a partner is available.
