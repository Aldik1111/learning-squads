# French preferences conversation — 15 minutes

Choose familiar or fictional hobbies. Give both people time to speak; do not pressure anyone to reveal personal information.

## Minutes 0–3: say what you like
Take turns saying two sentences with `J’aime...`. The listener repeats one activity they understood. Use `lire`, `marcher`, or `écouter de la musique` as starting points.

## Minutes 3–7: ask and contrast
Ask `Vous préférez lire ou marcher ?` and respond with `Je préfère...`. Add one respectful dislike using `Je n’aime pas...`. Practice the whole phrase again after a correction.

## Minutes 7–12: find common ground
Role-play choosing an activity for a break. Start with different preferences, offer a third activity, and confirm agreement. Swap roles and repeat with another pair of options.

## Minutes 12–15: reflect
Summarize the fictional agreement and ask for one useful correction. Write down your own improved phrase. Your task submission should contain your reflection, not another person's identity or a private call link.

Aim for at least 15 minutes of practice for this module, which can accumulate across sessions.
