# A guide for your Spanish conversation

Aim for at least 15 minutes of practice for this module. These sections are suggestions, not a booking schedule. Continue longer if you both want to.

1. **Get comfortable (about 2 minutes).** Check that you can hear each other. Say you are practising introductions. Agree that either person can ask for repetition.
2. **Introduce yourselves (about 4 minutes).** Take turns with `Me llamo...`, `¿Cómo te llamas?`, `Soy de...` and `¿De dónde eres?`. Use fictional details if you prefer.
3. **Try a short exchange (about 4 minutes).** Add `Me gusta cocinar`. Ask your partner for one other activity phrase. Repeat it and ask them to say it more slowly if needed.
4. **Switch roles (about 3 minutes).** Start again with the other person asking the questions. Try once without reading, then use your notes to help.
5. **Reflect (about 2 minutes).** Name one phrase you can now use and one you want to repeat next time. Thank your partner and say `Hasta luego`.

No partner available? Rehearse both parts and complete the knowledge questions; return to conversation practice later. There is no limit of one call per course. A team can also start a group call when two or more teammates choose to practise.

The Meet timer measures time since you clicked the link. Choose **Finish this practice** when you finish. Completed sessions linked to this module accumulate toward 15 minutes; overlapping intervals count only once. The application cannot confirm that someone joined or stayed in the call. Reaching 15 minutes does not by itself certify learning or attendance.
