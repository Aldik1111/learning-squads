# Start a conversation in Spanish

Your goal is to introduce yourself and keep a simple conversation going. Read and try the quiz now; you do not need to wait for a partner.

## Useful phrases

| Spanish | English |
| --- | --- |
| Hola. | Hello. |
| Me llamo Sam. | My name is Sam. |
| ¿Cómo te llamas? | What is your name? (informal) |
| Soy de Canadá. | I am from Canada. |
| ¿De dónde eres? | Where are you from? (informal) |
| Me gusta cocinar. | I like cooking. |
| ¿Puedes repetir, por favor? | Can you repeat, please? |
| Más despacio, por favor. | More slowly, please. |
| Gracias. | Thank you. |
| Hasta luego. | See you later. |

Replace the example name, place or activity with your own choice. Fictional details work too. `Me gusta` followed by a verb such as `cocinar` is one way to say you like doing something.

## Read a short exchange

**Sam:** Hola. Me llamo Sam. ¿Cómo te llamas?

**Alex:** Me llamo Alex.

**Sam:** ¿De dónde eres?

**Alex:** Soy de Canadá. Me gusta cocinar.

**Sam:** ¿Puedes repetir, por favor?

**Alex:** Me gusta cocinar.

**Sam:** Gracias.

Read both parts aloud. Then cover the English column and try introducing yourself. Getting stuck is a chance to practise asking for repetition.

## Prepare for practice

Pick a name, a place and an activity for the conversation. Keep two support phrases nearby: asking for repetition and asking someone to slow down. The accompanying conversation guide proposes at least 15 minutes of practice for this module.

Language preferences in a profile are self-reported. A Spanish-primary profile does not certify a person's teaching ability or native-speaker status. You can practise with your team too; you do not have to arrange a scheduled slot to read this lesson.

Vocabulary reference: [RAE dictionary: hola](https://dle.rae.es/hola). The dialogue, translations and activities are original examples.
