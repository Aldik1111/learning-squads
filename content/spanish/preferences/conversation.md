# Spanish preferences conversation — 15 minutes

Choose harmless fictional hobbies if you prefer. The goal is understandable exchange, not agreement on every preference.

## Minutes 0–3: introduce your choices
Each person gives two activities using `Me gusta...`. Ask `¿Y a ti?` after one sentence. The listener identifies one shared activity.

## Minutes 3–7: singular and plural
Practice `Me gusta el café` and `Me gustan los libros`, then substitute a familiar singular or plural noun. Ask your partner to explain one correction slowly. Return to the complete sentence after correcting it.

## Minutes 7–12: choose together
Offer a choice with `¿Prefieres... o ...?`. Your partner deliberately chooses a different first option. Find another proposal, respond politely, and confirm it. Swap roles so both people ask and answer.

## Minutes 12–15: recall and feedback
Retell your fictional agreement in two sentences. Ask for one useful correction. Note your own improved phrase and explain the gusta/gustan choice in your private task result.

Aim for at least 15 minutes of practice for this module, which can accumulate across sessions.
