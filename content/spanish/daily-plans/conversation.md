# Spanish daily-plans conversation — 15 minutes

Use fictional times and activities. Agree on informal address and ask your partner to speak slowly. You may split the practice across sessions; the intervals below are a suggested agenda, not an attendance test.

## Minutes 0–3: warm up
Greet each other. Say one plan using `Hoy voy a...` and another using `Mañana voy a...`. The listener repeats the activity they understood.

## Minutes 3–7: ask and clarify
Take turns asking `¿Qué vas a hacer mañana?` and `¿A qué hora?`. Use two different activities. Ask for repetition once with `¿Puedes repetir, por favor?` rather than guessing.

## Minutes 7–12: negotiate a change
Role-play a practice call. One person proposes six; the other can only do seven. State morning/evening and confirm the final day and time. Swap roles and choose a different activity. Discuss the time-zone detail in a shared language if needed.

## Minutes 12–15: repeat and reflect
Repeat the final exchange without reading every line. Ask for feedback on one understandable phrase and one phrase to improve. Write your own fictional dialogue afterward; do not record or identify the other person.

Aim for at least 15 minutes of practice for this module, which can accumulate across sessions.
